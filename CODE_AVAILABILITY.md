# Code availability

This public repository provides a curated review-stage snapshot of the original MATIC-Echo implementation, including the principal model, sampling, loss, and statistical-analysis components.

To preserve the integrity of the ongoing peer-review process and avoid distributing unverified experimental artifacts, the complete training entry points, checkpoint-resume logic, frozen experiment configurations, and learned model checkpoints are not included in the current snapshot.

The complete verified training and inference code, final configurations, environment details, and eligible pretrained checkpoints will be released in this repository upon acceptance of the associated manuscript. Where required by a journal, confidential access to the complete reproducibility package can be provided to editors and peer reviewers during assessment.

Datasets are not redistributed. EchoNet-Dynamic and CAMUS must be obtained from their official providers under their respective access conditions and licences.

Repository: https://github.com/NIisar-shah/MATIC-Echo
